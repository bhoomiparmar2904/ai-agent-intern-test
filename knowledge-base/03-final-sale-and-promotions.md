# Final Sale & Promotional Items

Items marked final-sale at purchase are not eligible for standard returns or exchanges.

## Exception: damaged or incorrect final-sale items
Final-sale status does not block review for items that arrived damaged or incorrect. See
`04-damaged-or-wrong-items.md` for the reporting window and process. These cases always
require human review before any approval.
