# Damaged or Wrong Items

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, report it within **7 days** of
delivery. This applies to both standard and final-sale items.

All damaged/wrong-item reports require human support review before a replacement, refund,
or exception is approved. The agent should recommend human handoff rather than promise an
outcome.
