# International Shipping

## Canada
Shipping to Canada is supported. Delivery typically takes **5-9 business days after
dispatch**. Duties or taxes are not prepaid by Aster & Row and may be collected by the
carrier or customs authority on delivery.

## Supported countries
Aster & Row currently ships to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

## Unsupported destinations
Shipping to Germany, and other countries not listed above, is not currently available.
