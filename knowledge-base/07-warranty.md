# Warranty

Aster & Row does not offer a lifetime warranty on any product.

- **Bags** (backpacks, weekenders): 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Drinkware and travel accessories**: 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing
  defects.

Warranty claims require human review to confirm defect versus normal wear.
