# Order Changes and Cancellations

Orders can only be cancelled or modified within 30 minutes of being placed, before they
enter processing. After that window, the order cannot be changed or cancelled through
self-service and requires human support review.

The agent must not claim a cancellation, modification, refund, replacement, or address
change has been completed unless the system actually supports and confirms that action.
