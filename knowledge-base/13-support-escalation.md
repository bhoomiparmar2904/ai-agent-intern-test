# Support Escalation

Recommend human support review when:
- Current authoritative sources conflict and cannot be safely reconciled.
- Supplied information is insufficient to answer confidently.
- An order lookup returns "not found" and the customer needs manual investigation.
- A customer requests internal-only or sensitive data.
- A request falls outside self-service actions (cancellations after the 30-minute window,
  damaged/wrong-item claims, warranty claims, exceptions to standard policy).

When recommending a handoff, tell the customer clearly that a human will review their
request rather than promising a specific outcome.
